# Pocket Wilds - what is new

## [4.2.1] - 2026-10-31  -  clearer screen
- Red "!": what you already looked at is kept in the save, so a restart does not light all three again;
  BAG only for a stronger item that just came in (not ones already lying in the bag)
- Top panel: ATK / DEF / SPD always fit inside (big numbers short: 12.3K, 456K, 1.2M)
- Damage numbers vanish when they reach the HP bar, so the enemy's name stays readable
- "XP" label on the blue bar beside the monster's name

## [4.2.0] - 2026-10-31  -  save to a file, quieter "!"
- **Save to a file and back.** The web installer has two new buttons: "เก็บเซฟเป็นไฟล์" downloads the
  save, "คืนเซฟจากไฟล์" puts it back after another install (even after a full erase). The file is
  scrambled and signed, so it cannot be edited, and it only goes back onto the board it came from.
  Works in the give-away build ("@saveget" / "@saveput" over USB; tools/savefile.py does the same from a PC)
- **Red "!" only for something new.** Each reason shows once; opening its page puts it away until it
  goes and comes back: UPGRADE only while stuck on a stage (farming) with gold for a level, BAG when a
  stronger item comes in, BATTLE for Boss Rush ready, a friend nearby or the Tower 20% stronger
- sfttech2000 logo at boot: black screen, fades in and out (from cyd_libs, packed to 9 KB)
- Web installer: installs go through the page only (the one-file .bin download is gone)

## [4.1.0] - 2026-10-22  -  bag, gems, quests, Arena challenge
- **Arena: challenges are asked first.** Tapping a friend sends a request; the friend gets a
  CHALLENGE! popup on any page (name, monster, level, ATK, HP, 15 s) and taps FIGHT! to start
  right away, or NO. Boards on 4.0.0 do not see this version (rules changed)
- Battle page: blinking red "!" on each row with something to do (friends nearby, Boss Rush
  ready, Tower not tried yet); the Arena row shows how many friends are near and their names
- Fix: a friend's challenge while on another page fought out of sight; it now goes HOME
- **Refine stones only come from tapping the flying stone.** Check-in, quests and SALVAGE no
  longer give any (quests pay gold instead, SALVAGE pays gold); USB log prints every change
- Quests start with upgrades: ATK to 3 (sword), DEF to 3 (armor), speed to 3 (gloves), then
  equip, then the stage quests. A quest finished on the UPGRADE / BAG page goes back HOME and
  shows the reward at once
- **One kind of gem** (old grade III values): drops are shards, 9 shards make a gem by themselves.
  Gems page shows a shard bar per kind; the socket page lists only what you have (gems, then cards).
  SOCKET + button in the bag when the picked item has an empty slot; item details list all slot bonuses.
  Starter kit has one slot; the Lv10 quest gives an ATK gem and walks you through socketing it.
  Socket page shows the item and all its slots on top (the one being filled blinks; tap another
  slot to switch) and what the slots add.
  Old saves: grade I = 1 shard, II = 3 shards, III = 1 gem; small gems in items come back as shards
- **New bag**: 12 places each for swords, armor and gloves (4 x 3 under each worn slot), sorted by
  frame colour then strength. When a block is full the weakest unlocked item is sold for gold.
  LOCK / UNLOCK button; refined or socketed items lock themselves; locked items cannot be salvaged.
  Green arrow on items stronger than the worn one. Old saves: the 30-place bag is sorted into the blocks
- Tower reminder ("!"): before the first run, then again only once the pet is 20% stronger
  (ATK x HP) than at the start of the last run
- Check-in day 1 pays at least 1,000 gold: enough for ATK, DEF and speed to Lv3
- Quest chain: wear all 3 starter items, then catch a flying stone (one keeps flying round until
  it is caught, then the BAG shows how to refine), then refine one item to +1. After that stones
  come on the usual clock. Kill quests count from when the quest shows up. One Tower quest after
  stage 5 and one Boss Rush quest when it unlocks; no Arena quests (it needs a second board)
- Flying stones only come once the player owns an item
- The word on screen is "slot"; item details sum the same bonuses (two ATK gems = +50ATK)

## [4.0.0] - 2026-10-22  -  Pocket Wilds
- New name: **Pocket Wilds** (was CYD Ranger), by tee & claude. Title screen shows the six final
  forms with the wild monsters of the world in front
- Every monster form has real stand / walk / attack pictures (12 forms); the attack also fires
  an element shot (fire ball, water drop, leaf blade) with an element hit on the enemy
- Scrolling scenes: all 10 chapters are 3-part looping landscapes; after each win the pet walks
  on (steps follow the ground) and the next enemy comes in with the scene
- Beaten enemies vanish in a puff of white smoke; anime emotes over the pet (note, sweat, anger)
- Quest bar sized to its text
- Bigger game partition (partitions_cyd.csv); updating with "keep my save" keeps the save
- Painted sprites stored compressed (RLE); tools/cutout.py removes painted checkerboards
- **Fix: "Flash - keep my save" really keeps the save now.** The full image has blank 0xFF over
  the save area (0x9000-0xDFFF), so writing it at 0x0 wiped the save in every earlier package.
  Option 1 now writes update/boot_0x0.bin and update/game_0xe000.bin around the save (tested)
